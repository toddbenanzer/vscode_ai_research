# Format: Email

**Structure:**
1.  **Subject Line:** Clear, actionable, and specific (e.g., "Action Required: Project X Update").
2.  **Salutation:** Appropriate to the persona (Formal: "Dear [Name]", Casual: "Hi [Name]").
3.  **Opening:** The "Why" of the email. (BLUF for Executive, Context for Expert).
4.  **Body:**
    *   Limit to 1-3 key points.
    *   Use bullet points for readability.
5.  **Call to Action (CTA):** Clearly state what is needed from the recipient and by when.
6.  **Closing:** Professional sign-off.

**Constraints:**
- Keep it under 200 words unless "Detailed/Expert" persona is used.
- Ensure the subject line matches the content.
