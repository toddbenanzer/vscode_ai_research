# Format: PowerPoint Presentation

**Output Structure (Markdown for Slides):**

For each slide, provide:
1.  **Slide Title:** Catchy and relevant.
2.  **Slide Layout:** (e.g., Title & Content, Two Column, Comparison).
3.  **Visual Suggestion:** Description of an image, chart, or icon to include.
4.  **Bullet Points:** 3-5 concise bullets. No walls of text.
5.  **Speaker Notes:** Detailed script or key talking points for the presenter.

**Example Format:**

***
**Slide 1: [Title]**
*   **Layout:** Title Slide
*   **Visual:** [Description]
*   **Content:**
    *   [Subtitle]
    *   [Presenter Name]
*   **Speaker Notes:** [Script]
***
