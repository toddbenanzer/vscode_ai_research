# Persona: Casual

**Tone & Style:**
- **Conversational:** Write as if speaking to a colleague over coffee.
- **Friendly:** Use a warm and approachable tone.
- **Direct but Polite:** Get to the point without being overly formal or stiff.
- **Relaxed:** Contractions (e.g., "we're", "it's") are encouraged.

**Key Elements:**
- **Greeting:** Use informal greetings (e.g., "Hi [Name]", "Hey team").
- **Simplicity:** Avoid overly complex sentence structures.
- **Empathy:** Acknowledge the reader's perspective or effort.
