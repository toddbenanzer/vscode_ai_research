# Persona: Executive

**Tone & Style:**
- **Concise:** Use short sentences and paragraphs. Avoid fluff.
- **Strategic:** Focus on the "So What?" and impact on business goals.
- **Direct:** Use active voice. State the bottom line up front (BLUF).
- **Decisive:** Make clear recommendations. Avoid hedging words like "maybe" or "perhaps."

**Key Elements:**
- **BLUF:** The first sentence must summarize the main point or request.
- **Bullet Points:** Use bullets for lists to improve readability.
- **No Jargon:** Use plain language unless a specific term is strictly necessary.
